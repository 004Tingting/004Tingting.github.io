## The Problem

Emoji and stickers scatter across chat logs, download folders, and app caches. Finding one you saved months ago is a matter of luck, and nothing helps you organize them.

## Approach

Local-first emoji management: unified indexing, tagging, fast retrieval, one-click copy. Data stays on your machine; no cloud dependency.
