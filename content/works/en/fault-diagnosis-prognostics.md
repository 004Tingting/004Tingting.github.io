## Context

Health management of machinery comes down to two questions: what is failing and how badly (diagnosis), and how much longer it can run (remaining useful life prediction). Engineering settings have hard requirements on interpretability and deployment cost — pure black boxes rarely survive contact with reality.

## Approach

- Feature extraction and time-frequency analysis of vibration and operating signals
- Data-driven fault classification
- Degradation trend modeling and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction

## Progress

- Built a diagnosis baseline on an engineering dataset
- Comparative experiments on features and model choices
- Next: confidence intervals for predictions, and online updating
